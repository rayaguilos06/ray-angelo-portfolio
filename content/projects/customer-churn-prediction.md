Cleaned and explored a customer subscription dataset, engineered features such as usage frequency and support ticket history, and trained and compared logistic regression, random forest, and gradient boosting models. The final model was evaluated on precision and recall to balance the cost of missed at-risk customers against false alarms, and results were summarized in a short report with clear recommendations for the retention team.
